HOW IT WORKS

Two portals. One seamless process.

CareerBridge serves candidates and companies with completely separate, purpose-built experiences.

STEP 01

Create your profile

Build a complete professional profile with skills, education, experience, and projects.

STEP 02

Find & apply for jobs

Browse real job postings from companies and submit your application in seconds.

STEP 03

Take the assessment

Complete the company-designed MCQ test linked directly to your application.

STEP 04

Groom if needed

If you didn't pass, explore curated learning resources matched to the job's required skills.

STEP 05

Reattempt & qualify

Take a second attempt after grooming. Pass and your profile goes to the company.
